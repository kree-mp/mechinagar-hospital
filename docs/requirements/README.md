# Data Requirements — Mechinagar Hospital Website

Items needed from the client to complete the website. Each section below maps to a visible section on the site.

---

## 1. Logos & Official Assets

- **Government emblem image** — official Nepal government emblem (PNG/SVG, transparent background); currently shown as a placeholder box in the masthead
- **Nepal flag image** — for the masthead beside the date/time block; currently a placeholder stripe
- **Hospital logo** — if a custom logo exists (PNG/SVG, transparent background); otherwise the emblem is used

---

## 2. Social Media Links

Which platforms does the hospital use? Please provide the full profile URLs:

- Facebook page URL
- YouTube channel URL
- Twitter / X profile URL (if any)
- LinkedIn page URL (if any)

> If none exist, the social media buttons will remain hidden.

---

## 3. Hero / Banner Photos

Three full-width banner photos for the homepage slider:

| Slot | Subject |
|------|---------|
| 1 | Hospital exterior / front building |
| 2 | Emergency ward or ambulance |
| 3 | Lab / modern equipment / operation area |

- Format: JPG or WebP, landscape, minimum 1600 × 900 px

---

## 4. Gallery Photos & Videos

Photos (and optionally a video) for the gallery section:

| Slot | Subject |
|------|---------|
| 1 (large) | Hospital building — 2 or more photos |
| 2 | OPD / service delivery — 1–2 photos |
| 3 | Laboratory service — 1–2 photos |
| 4 | Inpatient ward — 1–2 photos |
| 5 | Staff group photo — 1 photo |
| 6 (wide) | Health camp — 1–2 photos |
| 7 | Programs & training — 1–2 photos |
| 8 | Video — YouTube or MP4 link (optional) |

- Format: JPG or WebP, minimum 800 × 600 px per photo

The department head's contact information (currently blank):

- Health Branch in-charge phone number
- Health Branch in-charge email address

## 7. News & Events

For each news item or event the hospital wants to publish, please provide:

- Date (BS or AD)
- Category / label (e.g., Health Camp, New Equipment, Awareness)
- Headline / title
- Short summary (2–3 sentences)
- Photo (optional, JPG/WebP, landscape)

> At least 3 recent items recommended so the section is not empty.

---

## 8. Reports & Publications (PDF Files)

Upload-ready PDF files for each report type:

- Monthly reports (मासिक प्रतिवेदन)
- Quarterly reports (त्रैमासिक प्रतिवेदन)
- Annual reports (वार्षिक प्रतिवेदन)
- Financial reports (आर्थिक प्रतिवेदन)
- Health statistics reports (स्वास्थ्य तथ्याङ्क प्रतिवेदन)

For each file: title, date/fiscal year, and the PDF itself.

---

## 9. Notice Board Content

Actual notices to publish (notices, procurement, vacancies). For each notice:

- Date (BS)
- Notice reference number
- Title
- PDF attachment (optional)
- Category: General Notice / Procurement / Vacancy & Results

> Sample notices are shown on the site now; these will be replaced with real ones.

---

## 10. Online Appointment / Complaint System

- Does the hospital have an online appointment system? If yes, provide the link.
- Does the hospital have a complaint/feedback system? If yes, provide the link or email.

> Currently both buttons link to the Contact section as a fallback.

---

## 11. Website Domain

- Desired domain name (e.g., `mechinagarhospital.gov.np`)
- Hosting provider preference (if any)

---

## 12. Staff Contact Details (Optional)

If the hospital wants individual phone/email shown per staff member, provide those. Currently only name and designation are displayed.
